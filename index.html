About

Bryce

I'm a photographer and creative director from Connecticut, shooting under the name 24HRT.Z.

I build every shoot around the light. Colored gels, cut shadows, a gas station at night, a record spinning on a coffee table. If it can throw color across a face, I'm using it.

I'm studying Communications at UConn and making spec campaigns for brands I want to work with, so you can see the idea instead of hearing about it. I'm headed for fashion, entertainment, and advertising.
